# Transferable Lessons from Doctoral Writing

These are **interpretive case notes**, not citations proving a gap in a new project. Verify original thesis passages, printed page numbers and complete bibliographic metadata before formal academic citation.

## NM-AIST examples
- **Lucas Paul (2022), cassava linamarase:** a missing experimental structure becomes a scientific problem only when linked to specific interactions or conformational inferences the current evidence cannot support; computational modelling alone does not demonstrate improved cassava processing outcomes.
- **Faraji, hydropower optimisation:** a one-factor-at-a-time limitation matters when interactions could change a meaningful scientific result.
- **Mbelele, drug-resistant TB:** connect limitations of existing diagnostics to the decisions and interpretations affected.
- **Mwapinga, blood-flow modelling:** newly combined physical factors require mechanistic or empirical justification.
- **Mlay, TB/migration modelling:** omitted population processes matter only if consequential to the chosen estimand.
- **Kato, machine learning and library information retrieval:** a real application problem does not automatically justify a favoured algorithm.
- **Ndakidemi, biological pest control:** do not assume evidence automatically transfers between systems.
- **Chacha, groundwater:** quantify a problem where appropriate, and avoid unsupported causal leaps.
- **Mayengo, alcohol-related mathematical modelling:** identify how omitted sociocultural drivers could change a model inference.
- **Mpenda, NDV susceptibility:** candidate-gene association is not causal resistance mechanism.
- **Mkonyi, human–carnivore conflict:** perceived and observed outcomes are not interchangeable.

## MIT, Imperial, Stanford
- **Kazemian (Stanford, 2013):** a short problem paragraph can be supported by surrounding motivation and formalisation.
- **Boyce (Stanford, 2007):** investigate model assumptions using alternative explanations and discriminating evidence.
- **Phillips (Imperial, 2023):** computational speed matters relative to physical fidelity and intended use.
- **Mo (Imperial, 2026):** formulate measurable interpretability–reconstruction/measurement trade-offs. **Abstract-level prior appraisal**.
- **Ilyas (MIT, 2025):** benchmark performance and predictability of failure are distinct. **Abstract-level prior appraisal**.
- **Wu (MIT, 2025):** prediction alone does not identify causal effects. **Abstract-level prior appraisal**.

## Cambridge chemistry and AI
- **Chen Gong (2023):** acknowledge valid predictive achievements before addressing uncertainty and interpretability.
- **Benoit Baillif (2024):** compare method trade-offs and limitations of evaluation metrics, not merely method names.
- **Maria-Anna Trapotsi (2021):** link assay/label uncertainty to a testable question; distinguish experimentally measured predictors from readily computable ones.
